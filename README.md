## Project Overview

**Gravity in a Bottle** is an interactive story and arcade exploration experience set aboard the stranded deep-space vessel **Odyssey-6**. 

Nineteen days into deep-space transit, Odyssey-6 loses all contact with Earth. Cut off from Mission Control, the six-person crew turns to the ship’s onboard **Archive Terminal**—studying the real landers, rovers, helicopters, and atmospheric probes humanity has sent to other worlds and left behind on their surfaces, in their clouds, and inside their impact craters. By reliving these historic missions and learning how past robotic pioneers survived extreme environments and vast distances, the crew uncovers the clues needed to restore their backup communications dish and signal home.

Grounded in verified mission records and photography from NASA, ESA, and partner space programs, **Gravity in a Bottle** blends character-driven storytelling, hands-on arcade simulations, and an interactive space-history Q&A assistant into an accessible experience for curious explorers of all ages.

## Structure of the Experience

1. **Odyssey-6 Story & Crew Deck**: Meet the six crew members and ship AI through retro pixel-art dialogue scenes as they confront nineteen days of radio silence and begin searching the ship’s archives for answers.
2. **The Orbital Radar & Solar System Map**: Explore an interactive map spanning **Mercury, Venus, Earth, the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Titan, Uranus, and Neptune**, where glowing beacons highlight worlds with active field simulations.
3. **Odyssey-6 Archive AI (Interactive Q&A Terminal)**: Chat directly with the ship’s onboard Archive Terminal to ask questions about lost, crashed, retired, or abandoned spacecraft and scientific instruments across space.
4. **Planetary Dossiers & Relic Logs**: Inspect each world’s extreme climate, geology, and the exact human-made hardware resting there today—including untouched ice giants Uranus and Neptune that have only ever been visited by a brief flyby.
5. **Field Simulations (7 Multi-Stage Missions)**:
   - **Mercury | MESSENGER**: Keep a ceramic sunshade locked against 430 °C solar glare, scan shadowed polar ice craters, and guide its 2015 impact into Shakespeare Crater.
   - **Venus | Venera 13**: Survive 92-bar sulfuric acid clouds, deploy an aerobraking ring, and drill volcanic basalt on Phoebe Regio before internal heat reaches 465 °C.
   - **The Moon | Lunokhod 1 & Surveyor 3**: Drive an 8-wheeled solar rover across Mare Imbrium using subsurface sonar, and operate Surveyor 3’s robotic soil scoop in Oceanus Procellarum ahead of the Apollo 12 rendezvous.
   - **Mars | Ingenuity**: Pilot a lightweight coaxial helicopter through thin Martian air and shifting Jezero Crater winds across three aerial scouting campaigns.
   - **Jupiter | Galileo Atmospheric Probe**: Plunge at 170,000 km/h into a 22-bar crushing abyss, tune radio bands inside the orbiter relay beam, and dodge super-bolt lightning.
   - **Saturn & Titan | Huygens Lander**: Descend through Titan’s orange methane haze, deploy parachutes below 130 km, shield against storm surges, and touch down on Xanadu’s shoreline.
6. **Unlocked Archival Telemetry & Finale**: Completing simulations unlocks real NASA and ESA mission photography and field reports. Clearing at least four missions restores Earth’s orbital station link, triggering the crew’s final transmission home.

## Experience Highlights

- **Story-Driven Space History**: Connects an emotional human survival story with real robotic exploration milestones across the Solar System.
- **Authentic Mission Challenges**: Every simulation reflects the real conditions of its destination—from thin-air flight on Mars and crushing atmospheric pressure on Jupiter to extreme heat on Venus and Mercury.
- **In-Character Archive Assistant**: Ask open-ended questions about abandoned probes, lunar rovers, and historic space hardware right from the radar screen.
- **Curated NASA & ESA Photography**: Every planet profile and completed mission unlocks real archival imagery and fascinating facts verified against official space agency records.
- **Playable Anywhere**: Works seamlessly across desktop and mobile screens with keyboard, mouse, and touch controls, automatically saving your mission progress as you explore.
